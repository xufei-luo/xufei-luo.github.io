Research

Research interests.

My research focuses on the methodological path from clinical evidence to trustworthy health decisions.

1. Clinical evidence synthesis and treatment decision-making

Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, evidence mapping, and dose-response analyses for clinically relevant treatment questions.

  1. Luo X, Zhu D, Li J, Ren M, Liu Y, Si T, et al. Selection of the optimal dose of sertraline for depression: a dose-response meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. Psychiatry Res. 2023;327:115391. doi:10.1016/j.psychres.2023.115391
  2. Luo X, Lv M, Zhang X, Estill J, Yang B, Lei R, et al. Clinical manifestations of COVID-19: an overview of 102 systematic reviews with evidence mapping. J Evid Based Med. 2022;15(3):201-215. doi:10.1111/jebm.12483

2. Infectious diseases, public health emergencies, and rapid guidance

Rapid evidence assessment and guideline development for COVID-19, respiratory disease, and other public-health emergencies.

  1. Luo X, Wang X, Long X, Ren M, Zhang X, Liu Y, et al. Supportive care for patient with respiratory diseases: an umbrella review. Ann Transl Med. 2020;8(10):621. doi:10.21037/atm-20-3298
  2. Chinese Society of Respiratory Diseases, Chinese Medical Association; Respiratory Physicians Branch of the Chinese Medical Doctor Association. Guidelines for the Diagnosis, Treatment, and Prevention of COVID-19 in Adults in China. Natl Med J China. 2021.

3. Clinical practice guideline methodology, evaluation, and reporting

Methods for guideline development, appraisal, reporting, registration, adaptation, implementation, and transparency.

  1. Luo X, Liu Y, Ren M, Zhang X, Janne E, Lv M, et al. Consistency of recommendations and methodological quality of guidelines for the diagnosis and treatment of COVID-19. J Evid Based Med. 2021;14(1):40-55. doi:10.1111/jebm.12419
  2. Luo X, Akl EA, Zhu Y, Lv M, Liu X, Song Y, et al. Developing the RIGHT extension statement for practice guideline protocols: the RIGHT-P statement protocol. F1000Res. 2022;11:275. doi:10.12688/f1000research.109547.1

4. Generative AI and large language models in evidence-based medicine

Responsible use, evaluation, reporting, and governance of LLMs across evidence synthesis and guideline workflows.

  1. Luo X, Tham YC, Giuffrè M, Ranisch R, Daher M, Lam K, et al; GAMER Working Group. Reporting guideline for the use of Generative Artificial Intelligence tools in MEdical Research: the GAMER Statement. BMJ Evid Based Med. 2025;30(6):390-400. doi:10.1136/bmjebm-2025-113825
  2. Luo X, Chen F, Zhu D, Wang L, Wang Z, Liu H, et al. Potential roles of large language models in the production of systematic reviews and meta-analyses. J Med Internet Res. 2024;26:e56780. doi:10.2196/56780

5. Evidence-based traditional Chinese medicine and integrated care

Clinical evidence, reporting standards, guideline localization, and knowledge translation for traditional Chinese medicine and integrated care.

  1. Luo X, Ni X, Lin J, Zhang Y, Wu L, Huang D, et al. The add-on effect of Chinese herbal medicine on COVID-19: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Phytomedicine. 2020;153282. doi:10.1016/j.phymed.2020.153282
  2. Luo X, Zhang Y, Li H, Ren M, Liu Y, Liu Y, et al. Clinical evidence on the use of Chinese herbal medicine for acute infectious diseases: an overview of systematic reviews. Front Pharmacol. 2022;13:752978. doi:10.3389/fphar.2022.752978
