01 About

Making health evidence more rigorous, useful, and transparent.

I am a researcher in evidence-based medicine and public health. My work develops and evaluates methods that help turn research evidence into trustworthy clinical guidance, with a growing focus on the role of large language models in this process.

My experience spans evidence synthesis, reporting guidance, health policy, traditional Chinese medicine, and international research collaboration. I am based at Hong Kong Baptist University.
