Xufei Luo, MPH, PhD, Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Hong Kong Baptist University.

My research primarily focuses on the intersection of evidence-based medicine and artificial intelligence. Addressing fragmented evidence, uneven research quality, insufficient efficiency in evidence production, and inadequate clinical translation in the prevention and treatment of major diseases, I have progressively developed three mutually supporting research directions: building an evidence-based knowledge system and clinical decision-making paradigm for major infectious disease prevention and control; building an innovation ecosystem integrating trustworthy artificial intelligence and evidence-based science; and building a system for evidence innovation and global translation in the modernisation of Chinese medicine. Driven by clinical and public health needs, my research combines evidence synthesis, methodological appraisal, guideline development, and AI tool development to translate research evidence into trustworthy knowledge, standardised guidelines, and decision-support tools.

I have a multidisciplinary educational and training background in clinical medicine, public health, Chinese medicine, and evidence-based medicine, and am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the School of Chinese Medicine, Hong Kong Baptist University. As of October 2026, I had published 124 English-language papers and 73 Chinese-language papers, contributed to the writing or translation of 11 books, and participated in more than 40 activities related to guidelines, standards, reporting guidelines, and consensus statements. As of 30 October 2026, my Google Scholar profile showed 3,189 citations and an H-index of 32. I was included in the 2026 Stanford University–Elsevier list of the world’s top 2% scientists and received honours including recognition among the top 5% of CNKI highly cited scholars in 2024 and 2025. I am the Principal Investigator of one National Natural Science Foundation of China Young Scientists Fund project. I currently serve as Founding Deputy Editor of AI Evidence-Based Medicine, Associate Editor of Systematic Reviews and the Journal of Migration and Health, and an Editorial Board Member of BMC Public Health. I have delivered more than 10 oral and poster presentations at meetings including Guidelines International Network conferences, Cochrane Colloquia, and the Global Evidence Summit.
