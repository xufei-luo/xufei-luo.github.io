Xufei Luo, MPH, PhD, is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Hong Kong Baptist University. His research centers on evidence-based medicine, clinical practice guideline methodology, reporting standards, and the responsible integration of large language models into medical research.

He works across the full path from clinical evidence synthesis to trustworthy guidance: developing and evaluating guidelines, improving reporting transparency, and translating evidence for public-health and clinical decisions.

Dr. Luo is a member of the WHO Collaborating Centre for Guideline Implementation and Knowledge Translation, Cochrane China, PREPARE, STAR, and RIGHT working groups. He has contributed to more than 50 clinical practice guidelines across diverse specialties.

He led the GAMER Statement, the reporting guideline for generative AI tools in medical research, and contributed to the RIGHT-P Statement. He serves as Associate Editor of Systematic Reviews and Journal of Migration and Health, and as an Editorial Board Member of BMC Public Health.
